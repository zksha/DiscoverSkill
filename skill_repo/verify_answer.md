## Answer Verification

Before committing to an answer, run a quick verification pass.

**Verification checklist:**
1. **Sanity check**: Does the answer make intuitive sense? Is it in the right ballpark?
2. **Units / format**: Is the answer in the format the question expects (number, name, date)?
3. **Reverse check**: Can you reconstruct the question from the answer?
4. **Edge case scan**: Are there obvious exceptions or corner cases that would invalidate the answer?
5. **Contradiction scan**: Does the answer contradict anything you stated earlier?

**Common error patterns to watch for:**
- Off-by-one errors in counting/sequence problems
- Confusing necessary vs. sufficient conditions
- Overlooking negations ("which is NOT...")
- Mixing up similar names, dates, or quantities

**Action on failure:** If verification fails, re-examine the reasoning step where the inconsistency originates.
