This skill assists the agent in strategically planning movements toward the win condition based on the layout of the rules and objects. The agent analyzes the available paths and employs logical reasoning to determine which moves will bring them closer to the 'WIN' block, whether by modifying rules, bypassing walls, or using other objects in creative ways. It involves assessing obstacles in relation to the goal and planning out a sequence of actions that leads efficiently to victory.
