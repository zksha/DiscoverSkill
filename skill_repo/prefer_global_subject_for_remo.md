When the nominal WIN target (e.g., FLAG) is unreachable—especially in two-room layouts—stop chasing it. Instead, list all noun-texts you can access and mark which have matching objects you can already touch or that exist on both sides (global terrain like WALL, DOOR, ROCK).

Choose the nearest viable noun and build '<NOUN> IS WIN' using any existing 'IS WIN' anchor or by assembling 'IS' and 'WIN' yourself. Prioritize the subject that requires the fewest pushes and does not force you through STOP barriers. Keep the 'IS WIN' pair anchored while you ferry only the subject into place.

Once the rule is formed, immediately win by touching that subject near you (e.g., make WALL IS WIN and step onto any wall). This bypasses blocked corridors and eliminates the need to enter the remote room.
