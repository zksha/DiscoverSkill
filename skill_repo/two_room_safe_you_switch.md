---
name: two_room_safe_you_switch
description: In two rooms, reassign YOU to a reachable noun without dropping control.
---

Detect a two-room split: your current YOU cannot path to the goal due to STOP walls and no editable rule can open a corridor from your side. Look for a noun-text you can reach that corresponds to objects positioned advantageously (often in the other room, or able to act remotely such as WALL, ROCK, DOOR).

Stage the switch safely. Keep at least one active '... IS YOU' at all times: place the chosen noun-text directly adjacent to the existing 'IS YOU' pair (left of a horizontal 'IS YOU' or above a vertical one), then make the final push to form '<NOUN> IS YOU'. Do not break 'BABA IS YOU' until the new rule is complete. Confirm control transfers to the new actor.

Use the new YOU to solve the blockage: navigate from the other room, flip a blocking rule there, or reach/touch WIN. If helpful, you can later dismantle the old YOU rule to prevent accidental control conflicts.
