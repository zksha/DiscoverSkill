name: two_room_switch_you_then_make_win
description: Switch YOU to a reachable noun in the target room, then make WIN.
---

If the 'IS WIN' anchor or your chosen subject is isolated in another room (separated by STOP walls or enclosure) and you cannot open a path quickly, switch control to a noun inside that room, then complete the win there.

Detection and choice:
- If there is no safe path from current YOU to the anchor/subject push-side, enumerate noun-texts inside the target room (same compartment as anchor). Pick a noun-text you can safely form "<NOUN> IS YOU" with, using available IS/YOU words.

Safe switching procedure:
- Maintain continuous control: create the new "<NOUN> IS YOU" before breaking the existing YOU sentence (or keep both temporarily). Avoid any move that leaves zero YOU.
- Once switched into the target room, use local text to assemble "<NOUN> IS WIN". Prefer using the existing 'IS WIN' anchor; otherwise, move IS/WIN to your subject and attach.

Cautions:
- Do not trap your new YOU behind STOP with no rule access. If needed, first break a local STOP in that room, then switch YOU, then attach the subject to WIN.
