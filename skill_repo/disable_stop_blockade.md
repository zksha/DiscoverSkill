When progress is blocked, scan for an active "X IS STOP" rule. Identify which of the three text blocks (X, IS, STOP) is movable and accessible from your side. Your cheapest unlock is to slide any one of these out of alignment to break the rule, turning the stopper traversable.

If you can’t reach the chain directly, look for indirect access: pull nearby text into position to nudge the chain, create an alternate YOU to approach from another angle, or temporarily rearrange harmless rules to clear workspace. Prioritize edits that require the fewest pushes and don’t endanger your "YOU" rule.

After breaking STOP, immediately capitalize: either cross the former barrier or repurpose the freed text. A common shortcut is to recycle the layout to build a win condition (for example, swinging WIN into place where STOP used to be). Re-check active rules after each change to avoid accidental re-formation of STOP.
