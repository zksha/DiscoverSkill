Scan for an 'IS WIN' pair already aligned in a row. Do not chase the 'WIN' text; instead, select the closest movable noun-text that can be attached to the left of 'IS WIN' with minimal moves.

Plan a vertical attach: position yourself beneath (or above) the noun-text so you can push it straight up (or down) until it slots as '<NOUN> IS WIN'. Vertical moves are safer and avoid sliding the anchor. Ensure there is empty space between the noun and the anchor and that you won’t shove the anchor out of alignment.

If a STOP barrier prevents reaching the noun or the anchor, first break it (use the STOP-breaking procedure) and then resume the vertical attach. While maneuvering, protect 'BABA IS YOU'—never push its IS/YOU out of place.

After forming the rule, immediately re-scan active WIN targets and walk to the nearest WIN f-object (often you have already won if you overlap it). If not, take the shortest unobstructed path to touch it.
