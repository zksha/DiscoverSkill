---
name: identify_block_types
description: Classify blocks by their properties (e.g., movable, stop) to plan actions.
---

Understanding the types of blocks present in the environment is crucial for effective movement and strategy in puzzle-solving. Classifying blocks as movable (like text rules) or immovable (like walls) allows you to strategize accordingly. When entering a new environment, take a moment to observe and mentally note the types of blocks. This groundwork prepares you for the next steps, as you can formulate a plan based on how obstacles or rules can be manipulated. Always reassess block types before deciding your next move.
