Diagnose blockage: if your current YOU cannot traverse a STOP wall, a sealed corridor, or a room separation, plan to shift control. Identify a nearby noun-text X that can exist or be moved into the target area or across the barrier more easily than your current avatar.

Safely rewrite control by forming “X IS YOU” while preserving at least one YOU sentence during the transition to avoid losing. Prefer using spare copies of IS and YOU or temporarily moving text so that a YOU condition is always maintained. Once control is switched, use the new body to reach otherwise inaccessible text or tiles.

After crossing, either create a winning rule directly (e.g., “X IS WIN” or “FLAG IS WIN”) using accessible text, or re-establish “BABA IS YOU” if needed and then form the WIN rule. Avoid scattering critical text; move with purpose to complete the minimal rule chain.

Before finalizing, check that the newly controlled actor can still manipulate the necessary text. If not, revert or pick another candidate X that provides better leverage to complete the solve.
