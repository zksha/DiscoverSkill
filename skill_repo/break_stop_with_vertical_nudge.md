description: Break 'X IS STOP' via a single vertical nudge to clear access.
---

When a wall of STOP blocks your path to the anchor or subject, prioritize minimally breaking the STOP rule instead of wandering. Identify the horizontal (or vertical) sentence "X IS STOP". Your objective is to nudge either STOP or IS vertically by one tile to break the grammar.

Approach from the vertical push-side: stand below STOP and push up (or above and push down). If STOP’s vertical side is unreachable, do the same to the IS in that sentence. Avoid side-pushing within the sentence, which often preserves the rule. Critically, confirm you are not disturbing "BABA IS YOU" while doing this.

Once "X IS STOP" is broken, immediately resume your main plan (attach a subject to IS–WIN). Don’t over-edit: a single vertical nudge that opens a passage is ideal; leave extra text pieces in place to reduce backtracking.
