description: Select a reachable noun-text near 'IS WIN' and commit to attaching it.
---

When no WIN rule is active, first decide which noun-text will become the subject of "IS WIN". Avoid flip-flopping between candidates. Choose one noun-text that you can actually attach to the anchor soon, then commit until attached.

Procedure:
- Enumerate all noun-text tiles (ignore f-objects like fball/fkey — they are not text).
- Identify the 'IS WIN' anchor and its target subject cell (left of IS if horizontal; above IS if vertical).
- Filter out noun-texts that are in a different room or behind an immediate, nontrivial STOP barrier you cannot quickly break (e.g., a solid 'WALL IS STOP' with no access to its text).
- Prefer candidates that are in the same compartment as the anchor, already on (or close to) the anchor’s row/column, and have a clear push path to the target cell.

Commitment rule:
- Once chosen, store the subject and target cell. Do not chase other nouns unless this plan becomes impossible. All intermediate actions (including breaking STOP) should serve this single attachment goal.
