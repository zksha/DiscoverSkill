name: commit_to_subject_until_attachment
description: Lock onto one subject and finish the attachment before considering alternatives.
---

After selecting a noun-text to use with the IS–WIN anchor, commit to it. Record two things: the chosen subject and the anchor slot to occupy. Continue acting only if each move reduces the subject’s Manhattan distance to that slot or positions you on the correct push-side to do so.

Avoid oscillation and target-switching. Do not chase different nouns or poke the anchor mid-plan. Only reconsider if progress is impossible (e.g., subject fenced with no way to break STOP or no push-side access). In that case, first try to open access (e.g., use the STOP-breaking skill) or move the alternate nearest viable subject.

Use a simple loop: if not on push-side, route to push-side; else push one step toward the slot; re-evaluate; repeat. This monotonic approach prevents dithering and turns a high-level idea into a concrete, finishable sequence.
