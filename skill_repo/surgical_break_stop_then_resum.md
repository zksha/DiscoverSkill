name: surgical_break_stop_then_resume_goal
description: If STOP blocks progress, nudge IS/STOP one tile to break it, then continue.
---

When an 'X IS STOP' sentence creates a blocking wall/column between you and your objective (anchor or WIN object), perform a minimal, safe disruption of that sentence instead of aimless detours.

Procedure:
- Diagnose the blockade: confirm 'X IS STOP' is active and that STOP-labeled tiles are directly blocking the shortest route to your target (anchor or WIN f-object).
- Identify the sentence on the grid and choose the least risky nudge: prefer pushing STOP or IS vertically by one tile to break adjacency (so the rule deactivates) without scattering the text. Pick the direction with a free space and minimal collateral (never jeopardize 'BABA IS YOU').
- Route to the correct pushing side, execute a single nudge to break the rule, and immediately verify that 'X IS STOP' is no longer active.
- Resume your original plan: cross the previously blocked area to either attach your chosen noun to 'IS WIN' or to reach the WIN object. Do not keep pushing text after the blockade is gone unless required.

This targeted edit replaces dithering with a one-move fix, opening the path while preserving other crucial rules.
