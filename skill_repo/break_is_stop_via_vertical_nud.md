---
name: break_is_stop_via_vertical_nudge
description: Break 'X IS STOP' by nudging IS or STOP vertically; route to the push side.
---

Identify a complete 'X IS STOP' sentence. For a horizontal sentence, prefer popping the center IS or the right STOP one cell up or down; for a vertical sentence, pop left or right. Choose the word with a free cell orthogonally out of the sentence (above/below for horizontal; left/right for vertical).

Plan your approach: to push a block upward you must stand directly below it (and analogously for other directions). Route specifically to the push-side cell next to the chosen word, not to the sentence head. Execute exactly one push to dislodge that word and immediately confirm the rule deactivates ('X IS STOP' no longer listed).

If no orthogonal space exists to pop a word, first create space by sliding the sentence one step along its axis (push the leftmost/rightmost word sideways for horizontal, or top/bottom word for vertical) until an orthogonal cell opens, then perform the vertical (or horizontal) nudge. Avoid disturbing '... IS YOU' while doing this.

After the rule breaks, immediately exploit the opening: pass through the former STOP barrier or repurpose the freed words (e.g., turn 'WALL IS STOP' into 'WALL IS WIN').
