---
name: manipulate_rule_definitions
description: Change existing rules to open pathways or create new win conditions.
---

This skill allows the agent to manipulate the definitions of rules on the map. By moving around 'is', 'win', 'baba', and other key rules, the agent can redefine the properties of objects or the win conditions itself. A well-timed manipulation can open up paths previously blocked by walls or create alternative routes to reach the win condition. Analyzing surrounding blocks and potential interactions is key to successfully employing this skill.
