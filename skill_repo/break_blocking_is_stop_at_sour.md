---
name: break_blocking_is_stop_at_source
description: When a wall column blocks you, dislodge 'IS' or 'STOP' from 'X IS STOP'.
---

Detect a continuous barrier tagged STOP (e.g., vertical wall column). Instead of walking alongside it, locate the corresponding 'X IS STOP' sentence in the level's text cluster.

Choose the quickest single dislodge: prefer pushing STOP or IS vertically (up/down) one tile to break the sentence. Vertical dislodges minimize collateral rule formation. If one side is obstructed, consider dislodging the other word.

Route precisely to the push side: move to a tile directly above or below the chosen word with a free escape space on the opposite side. Avoid bumping 'BABA', 'IS', or 'YOU' in your control sentence. If a vertical approach is blocked, laterally nudge the sentence first to clear space, then perform the vertical pop.

Execute the single push to break the rule, then immediately test the barrier: if walls are no longer STOP, cross the former blockade. If STOP persists (e.g., multiple copies of the rule), repeat on the remaining sentence instances.
