---
name: select_viable_subject_text
description: Select the closest movable noun-text as subject for a new 'X IS WIN' rule.
---

List all noun-text blocks on the map (e.g., BABA, WALL, BALL, KEY). Prefer subjects that are not currently essential to 'YOU' (avoid disturbing 'BABA IS YOU' unless it is safe and clearly shortest). When possible, pick a subject that already has nearby access to the 'IS' and 'WIN' blocks or an existing 'IS WIN' pair.

Evaluate viability: the subject should be reachable, pushable along a clear corridor to the target slot, and not trapped behind STOP unless you can break that STOP rule first. Favor the subject requiring the fewest pushes and the least collateral movement of other rules.

If multiple candidates tie, prefer a subject whose physical objects are accessible after the rule forms (e.g., forming 'WALL IS WIN' when walls separate rooms may be useless). Only consider 'BABA IS WIN' as a fallback if it doesn't break 'BABA IS YOU' or if making yourself WIN will immediately end the level.

Commit to the chosen subject and ignore distracting f-objects (physical BALLs, etc.) until the rule is complete. This prevents wandering and keeps you focused on constructing the win condition.
