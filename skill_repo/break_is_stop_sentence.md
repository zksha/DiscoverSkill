---
name: break_is_stop_sentence
description: Break 'X IS STOP' by pushing STOP/IS vertically out of the sentence.
---

When a wall of STOP blocks progress, the cheapest fix is to break the rule text "X IS STOP". You almost never need to move the whole stack—just push one word (preferably STOP) out of alignment vertically so the rule deactivates.

Procedure:
- Locate any active "IS STOP" rule and find its text blocks on the grid. If horizontal, prefer pushing STOP up/down; if that space is blocked, try pushing IS up/down. If the rule is vertical, prefer pushing any word sideways.
- Choose the word with a free cell in the push direction (no STOP, no wall edge). Compute the approach cell opposite the push (to push STOP up, you must stand below STOP).
- Route to that approach cell without walking into STOP. Push once to break the sentence, then immediately reassess paths—the blocked corridor should now open.

Safety notes: Avoid shoving "BABA/IS/YOU" out of alignment unless you already have an alternate YOU. After breaking STOP, consider reusing the freed text (e.g., WALL/IS) to help assemble a WIN rule.
