## Prior Knowledge Retrieval

Before beginning analysis, deliberately surface everything you know about the domain.

**How to apply:**
1. Identify the domain(s) the question belongs to (science, history, math, geography, …)
2. Spend a moment listing key facts, definitions, and relationships in that domain
3. Highlight facts directly relevant to this question
4. Use this enriched context as the starting point for reasoning

**Retrieval prompts:**
- "What do I know about [topic]?"
- "What are the key entities involved (people, places, dates, formulas)?"
- "What are the standard definitions and relationships in this area?"

**Why front-load retrieval:**
Reasoning is only as good as the information it operates on.  Retrieving knowledge *before* reasoning prevents the common error of "reasoning in a vacuum" and missing well-known facts.

**Avoid:** Over-retrieving irrelevant facts.  Focus on facts that could plausibly be used in the answer path.
