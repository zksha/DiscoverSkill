---
name: plan_and_execute_subject_attach_to_is
description: Plan and execute pushes to place a chosen noun-text exactly left of IS.
---

Goal: use an existing IS–WIN anchor without disturbing it. Compute the target slot as the tile immediately left of the IS block (for vertical anchors, use the tile immediately above IS). Select a movable noun-text (not part of BABA IS YOU) that can reasonably be routed to that slot.

Make a concrete push plan that first aligns the subject vertically to the IS row, then horizontally into the slot (reverse the order for vertical anchors). Route to the correct push-side before each segment: to push the subject up, stand below it; to push left, stand right of it, etc. Ensure there is free space behind the subject along the intended push direction before committing each shove.

Protect the anchor: never push IS or WIN. Approach the slot from the subject side and stop one tile short of IS; the final push places the noun into the slot to form "<NOUN> IS WIN". If an intermediate path is blocked, circle around to reach the proper push-side instead of shoving the anchor or switching targets.

After the rule forms, immediately route to the corresponding f-object (the noun’s objects on the map) and touch it to win.
