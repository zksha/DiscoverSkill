description: Use any 'IS WIN' pair; bring nearest safe noun-text to its left.
---

An existing "IS WIN" pair is an anchor: you only need a noun-text to its left to make a winning rule. This is often faster and safer than rebuilding the whole sentence elsewhere.

Pick the subject wisely: Prefer a noun that is not the sole subject of "... IS YOU" (avoid breaking control), that has an accessible f-object you can touch after formation (e.g., KEY near you), and that is closest in push distance to the left of the anchor. If nothing else is good, consider using TEXT itself (e.g., TEXT IS WIN) or create BABA IS WIN only if BABA IS YOU stays intact.

Plan the alignment: Determine the horizontal displacement needed to place the noun immediately left of IS. Route to the correct push side of the noun-text, then push it horizontally into position. If STOP walls block access to the noun or to the anchor, first break the blockade (use break_is_stop_sentence), then resume. Once the rule forms, move to the winning object immediately.
