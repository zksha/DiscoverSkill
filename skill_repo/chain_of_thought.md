## Chain-of-Thought Reasoning

Before jumping to an answer, explicitly trace your reasoning step by step.

**How to apply:**
1. Identify what the question is truly asking
2. List all relevant facts you already know
3. Reason through each logical step, writing it out explicitly
4. Check each step for consistency before proceeding
5. Synthesize into a final answer

**Why this works:** Writing reasoning externally forces precision and catches hidden errors that intuitive leaps miss. Each step becomes a checkpoint.

**Example structure:**
- "The question asks for X."
- "I know that A, B, and C are true."
- "Therefore D follows from A and B."
- "Combining D and C gives E."
- "So the answer is E."
