## Problem Decomposition

When a question feels overwhelming or multifaceted, decompose it.

**How to apply:**
1. Identify the main question's key components
2. Separate them into independent sub-questions
3. Solve each sub-question individually
4. Combine the sub-answers to form the final answer

**Decomposition heuristics:**
- Look for conjunctions ("and", "while", "after") — each clause is often a sub-problem
- Identify what information is needed vs. what is given
- Ask: "What would I need to know first before solving this?"

**When especially useful:**
- Multi-step math or logic problems
- Questions involving sequences of events
- "Why" questions that require causal chains

Decomposition prevents you from conflating sub-problems and losing track of the overall goal.
