---
name: create_alternate_win_conditions
description: Manipulate rules creatively to form new 'win' conditions when blocked.
---

In cases where the straightforward win condition is blocked by obstacles, exploring alternate win scenarios is key. This involves analyzing the existing rules and objects; for instance, if 'key' can unlock something or a rule can be manipulated to redefine what 'win' means. Look for opportunities to use objects like 'key' to unlock doors or turn other elements into winning conditions. This creative restructuring can often bypass seemingly impenetrable barriers and lead to winning scenarios.
