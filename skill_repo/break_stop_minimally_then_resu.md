description: Detect a STOP wall blocking progress; break 'X IS STOP' with one nudge, then continue.
---

First, check whether your route to the goal (WIN object or IS-WIN anchor) is sealed by a continuous line of STOP objects and whether a matching "X IS STOP" rule is active. If yes, do not keep pathing toward the blocked target—pivot to disabling that STOP rule with the smallest possible change.

Prefer to dislodge STOP (first choice), then IS (second), then the subject noun X (last). Plan a one-tile nudge that breaks alignment: approach the chosen word from a side that has a free square to push it out of its sentence row/column. Favor vertical nudges (push up or down) if the trio is horizontal, or horizontal nudges if it is vertical. Ensure you do not disturb "BABA IS YOU" while maneuvering.

Execute the single nudge to break the rule, then immediately verify that "X IS STOP" disappeared from the active rules. If still active (e.g., duplicate STOP/IS nearby), repeat with one more minimal nudge until the blockade is disabled.

Once passable, resume your original plan (e.g., attach a noun to IS-WIN, or head straight to a WIN object). Do not continue shoving STOP text further than necessary; breaking the rule is the goal, not relocating the sentence.
